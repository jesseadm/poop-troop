## 💰 How Money Flows

### Revenue: $280/month (with 8 customers on Regular plan)

**Customer Pays:**
```
Month 1: Gets 30 days free
Month 2: $35 charged (via Stripe)
Month 3: $35 charged (via Stripe)
...continues monthly
```

**You Receive:**
```
Stripe Account → Your Business Bank
- $35 per customer
- Charged on billing date
- View in Stripe Dashboard
- Minus Stripe fee (2.9% + $0.30)
```

**You Pay Kids:**
```
Your Bank → Venmo App
- $35 per service completed
- Pay immediately after service
- Kids see instant notification
- Can withdraw to their bank
```

**Your Profit:**
```
Customer pays: $35/month
Kid gets paid: ~$30/month per service
Your margin: ~$5-10 per service (for routing, admin, etc)

With 8 customers on Regular (4 per kid per month):
- Revenue: $280/month
- Worker payouts: ~$240/month
- Your profit: ~$40/month (after Stripe fees)
```
